Laplace Research develops open research on probabilistic forecasting,
evidence-grounded reasoning, and belief revision in language models.
We are named for Pierre-Simon Laplace, who gave us the first serious
treatment of probability as a measure of what we don't know.

Our work centers on:

1. Training language models to produce calibrated probabilities rather than
   confident prose;
2. Building clean forecasting corpora — leakage detection, freeze-date splits,
   and auditing resolution quality at the source;
3. Reward design for post-training in domains where a forecast can be
   verifiably scored; and
4. Evaluation discipline: preregistration, correction for multiple comparisons,
   and publishing results that don't work.

## Research projects

- **Tarot-Draw** develops language models for calibrated probabilistic forecasting. [8B](https://github.com/Laplace-AI-Research/tarot-draw-8b) · [4B](https://github.com/Laplace-AI-Research/tarot-draw-4b) · [1.7B](https://github.com/Laplace-AI-Research/tarot-draw-1-7b)
- **ConvictionBench** evaluates whether language models revise their answers in response to evidence and how they respond to unsupported challenges. [Code and results](https://github.com/Laplace-AI-Research/conviction) · [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Laplace-AI-Research/ConvictionBench) · [Paper](https://convictionbench.org/paper.pdf)
- **Conviction** is a series of three experimental Qwen3-1.7B adapters for studying belief revision. [Anchor, Vector, and Meridian](https://huggingface.co/collections/Laplace-AI-Research/conviction-models-and-benchmark-6aff86d2ffe859af7052eafd)
