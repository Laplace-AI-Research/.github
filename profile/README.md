# Welcome to Laplace Research's Organization 👋

We build forecasting models: systems that put calibrated probabilities on
future events, and get scored on whether those probabilities hold up. Our
name is for Pierre-Simon Laplace, who gave us the first serious treatment
of probability as a measure of what we don't know.
